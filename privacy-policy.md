# Privacy Policy – Ka Audio Baibyl Katholik

_Last updated: 26 November 2026_

Ka Audio Baibyl Katholik ("the app") is a Catholic Bible app in the Khasi language, made by Marcel Myrthong. This page explains what the app does with your information.

## We do not collect your personal information

- The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in.
- The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information.
- The app has no advertising and no tracking or analytics tools.

## What stays on your phone

Bookmarks, notes ("Thoh") and downloaded audio chapters are saved only on your own phone. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you uninstall the app, they are deleted.

## Internet use

The app uses the internet only to play and download audio recordings of the Bible chapters. These recordings are stored on GitHub (github.com). When you play or download a chapter, your phone connects to GitHub to fetch the file, in the same way any web page loads. GitHub's own privacy statement is at https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement.

## Purchases

If the app offers a paid unlock, payment is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. The app only checks with Google Play whether the purchase was made, so that it can unlock the content.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

## Contact

If you have any questions, please write to us using the developer email address shown on the app's Google Play page.
